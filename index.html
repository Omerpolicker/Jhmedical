Questions patients ask

What is EZYPRO®?

EZYPRO® is an ECG recorder that records and stores every heartbeat, followed by a reporting service for your doctor. It helps healthcare providers personalise treatment, and ECG is the first-line test for diagnosing heart problems.

How long can I wear it?

Up to 14 days. Your physician will prescribe the right wear time for you, and actual wear time varies with lifestyle, environment and skin. If it comes off early, keep it safe and return it at your next appointment: all data recorded until then is saved and analysed.

What if my skin gets red or itchy?

EZYPRO® meets regulatory requirements and passed biocompatibility testing, but skin sensitivity differs. Some people feel temporary itching or swelling. Keep the area dry, stay somewhere cool and avoid sweaty activities. If irritation persists, consult a medical professional.

What should I do if I feel symptoms?

Press the blue button on EZYPRO®. The event is marked on your report, so your physician knows exactly when you felt it. Forgot to press? No problem: recording continues without interruption and no ECG data is missed.

Can I shower, exercise or travel?

Yes. EZYPRO® is waterproof, so you can shower and keep up light to moderate exercise. Avoid heavy sweating and stay cool and dry to complete your prescribed wear time. Don't take baths or go under water. You can pass airport security while wearing it.

Can I swim or use hot springs?

No. Avoid any underwater activity and don't spray water directly on the device. Excess water can shorten wear time and make the patch fall off early.
